# NETRAVAHA — Phase 8 Documentation: Investigation Command Center Experience

## 1. System Overview & Architectural Purpose

NETRAVAHA is a Unified CCTV Intelligence Platform built for the Gujarat Police Innovation Challenge 2026. Phase 8 transforms the platform's distributed camera network, AI vision processing, PostGIS spatio-temporal correlation, cryptographic evidence vault, and audit logging into a unified, institutional **Investigation Command Center**.

### Critical Institutional Disclaimer & Claim Discipline
> **IMPORTANT NOTICE:**  
> NETRAVAHA correlates **discrete CCTV sightings** across registered camera nodes using recorded timestamps, camera geospatial coordinates, and observed vehicle registration identifiers.  
> **It does NOT provide continuous GPS tracking, live continuous location, or exact road routes.**  
> The current system is an authorized CCTV intelligence prototype using controlled synthetic and research/demo video sources, with a production-oriented authorized RTSP/ONVIF/VMS ingestion boundary. It does not claim connection to 80,000 nationwide cameras or live state-level production networks.

## 4. Route Intelligence Terminology & Anomaly Rules

### Terminology Standards
- **Spatio-Temporal Sighting Correlation**: Mathematical correlation between discrete CCTV observations across distinct spatial coordinates and timestamps. Never labeled as "GPS track" or "live continuous location".
- **Geodesic Distance**: The shortest spherical distance across the WGS84 ellipsoid between camera coordinate pairs, computed via PostGIS `ST_Distance(geography, geography)`.
- **Estimated Average Velocity**: $v = \frac{\Delta d}{\Delta t}$ where $\Delta d$ is geodesic distance in kilometers and $\Delta t$ is elapsed time in hours between successive observations.

### Anomaly Classification Engine
The backend deterministic engine evaluates every sequential camera hop against physical transit constraints:

| Classification | Condition / Rule | Meaning to Police Investigator |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`PLAUSIBLE`** | Estimated speed $\le 120\text{ km/h}$ across inter-city highways, or $\le 60\text{ km/h}$ within city limits. | Transit between camera points is physically consistent with highway or arterial road speeds. |
| **`REQUIRES_REVIEW`** | Estimated speed between $120\text{ km/h}$ and $180\text{ km/h}$, or camera transit under 60 seconds with marginal velocity. | Unusual driving velocity or route shortcut. Requires human investigator review. |
| **`IMPOSSIBLE`** | Estimated speed $> 180\text{ km/h}$ (`UNREALISTIC_HIGH_VELOCITY`) or instantaneous cross-city teleportation ($\Delta t \approx 0$). | **Cloned license plate**, sensor clock drift, or ANPR OCR character confusion. The vehicle could not physically traverse the distance in the elapsed time. |

## 5. Evidence Integrity & Legal / Procedural Scope

### Terminology Standards
- **`EVIDENCE INTEGRITY` / `INTEGRITY VERIFIED`**: Validates that the SHA-256 hash computed directly from the byte stream retrieved from the MinIO object vault matches byte-for-byte with the stored SHA-256 digest recorded in PostgreSQL upon AI pipeline ingestion (`SHA-256 DIGEST MATCH`).
- **`STORED DIGEST` & `LIVE CALCULATED DIGEST`**: Exposes the recorded database digest and the runtime byte-level calculated digest.
- **Strict Avoidance of Unsupported Legal Claims**:
  - Never described as *"tamper-proof"* or *"immutable"* (as physical and infrastructure constraints exist in any software system).
  - Never described as *"Section 65B compliant"*, *"Section 65B certified"*, *"legally admissible"*, *"automatically admissible"*, or *"statutory certificate ready"*.

### Legal / Procedural Notice
Every evidence display and export package explicitly incorporates the following institutional notice:
> **LEGAL / PROCEDURAL NOTICE**  
> Cryptographic integrity verification confirms that the retrieved evidence object matches its recorded SHA-256 digest. This technical verification does not by itself establish legal admissibility, statutory compliance, authenticity, or evidentiary sufficiency. Applicable legal and departmental procedures must be followed independently.  
> **AUTHENTICITY / ADMISSIBILITY REQUIRES INDEPENDENT LEGAL AND PROCEDURAL REVIEW**

### Cryptographic Verification Pipeline
1. Sighting captured by AI vision worker $\rightarrow$ SHA-256 generated from raw image frame bytes.
2. Frame persisted to MinIO object vault $\rightarrow$ SHA-256 digest persisted to PostgreSQL `evidence` table.
3. Investigator inspects sighting in Command Center $\rightarrow$ backend downloads object from MinIO vault, computes runtime SHA-256 digest, and performs constant-time equality check against database record.
4. **Export Package**: Generates a ZIP bundle containing the raw JPEG frame, metadata JSON, and a technical verification certificate documenting hash comparison and officer custody metadata.

## 6. Watchlist & Alert Correlation

The Investigation Command Center correlates vehicles against active watchlists:
- **Watchlist Match**: If the target plate matches an active entry (e.g., `STOLEN_VEHICLE`, `WANTED_SUSPECT`), the Command Center displays an alert header with category, alert priority (`CRITICAL`, `HIGH`), observing camera, timestamp, and a direct link to the Live Alert Console.
- **No Watchlist Match**: Displays a calm, institutional badge: `NO WATCHLIST MATCH: Vehicle is not flagged in active state crime catalogs`.
- **Not Checked**: In the event alert query fails or is pending, displays `NOT CHECKED`.

## 9. Current Prototype Limitations

1. **Straight-Line Geodesic Segments**: Route correlation calculates straight-line spherical distances between camera points. It does not calculate turn-by-turn road topology (OSRM integration deferred to future phases).
2. **Camera Network Density**: Correlation resolution is proportional to camera density along physical roadway corridors.
3. **Synthetic / Research Video Boundary**: Video feeds use controlled synthetic video loops and research datasets conforming to the authorized ingestion boundary.
